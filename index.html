What we do all day, every day

Nails are the whole job here, not a sideline. Every service starts with clean, sanitised tools and ends with a finish we are happy to put our name on.

The little green salon by the marina

Clover Nail Salon And Spa sits in the Harbour Village shops on Harbour Village Drive, a short walk from the Gulf Harbour marina. Locals from across the Whangaparaoa Peninsula come in for nails that are done carefully, not quickly.

You will not be rushed out of the chair. The team checks shape and length with you before any colour goes on, and fixes anything you are not sure about before you leave.

  • Locally owned, part of the Harbour Village shops
  • Sanitised tools and fresh files for every client
  • Bookings by phone, walk-ins welcome when a chair is free
